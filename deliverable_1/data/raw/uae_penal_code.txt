UAE FEDERAL PENAL CODE
Federal Decree-Law No. 31 of 2021
On the Issuance of the Crimes and Penalties Law

================================================================================

PART ONE: GENERAL PROVISIONS

CHAPTER 1: Scope and Application

Article 1:
This Law shall be known as the "Crimes and Penalties Law." It shall apply to all crimes committed within the territory of the United Arab Emirates, including its land territory, territorial waters, airspace, and the continental shelf. The provisions herein replace Federal Law No. 3 of 1987 and its amendments. This Law aims to protect public order, safeguard individual rights, and preserve the security and stability of society.

Article 2:
The provisions of this Law shall apply to any person who commits a crime within the territory of the State, regardless of nationality, subject to the provisions of international treaties and agreements ratified by the State. Diplomatic immunity shall be respected in accordance with international law and the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations.

Article 3:
This Law shall apply to crimes committed outside the State in the following cases:
(a) If the crime is committed on board a vessel or aircraft registered in the State.
(b) If the perpetrator is a public official or a person assigned to a public service and the crime was committed during or because of the performance of their duties.
(c) If the perpetrator is a UAE national and the act is punishable under both this Law and the law of the country in which it was committed, provided the perpetrator is present in the State.
(d) Crimes against the State's internal or external security, crimes of counterfeiting, or crimes relating to currency forgery.

Article 4:
No crime shall exist and no penalty shall be imposed except by virtue of a law that was in force at the time the act was committed. A law shall apply retroactively only if it contains provisions that are more favorable to the accused. If a law issued after the commission of the crime and before a final judgment abolishes the criminality of the act, the case shall be dismissed and the execution of the penalty shall cease.

Article 5:
A person shall be presumed innocent until proven guilty by a competent court in a fair trial. The burden of proof lies with the prosecution.

CHAPTER 2: Criminal Responsibility

Article 6:
Criminal responsibility is personal. No person shall be punished for a crime committed by another. Each individual bears responsibility only for their own acts or omissions that constitute a criminal offence under this Law.

Article 7:
Criminal responsibility shall not apply to:
(a) A person who has not attained the age of criminal responsibility as determined by the Juveniles Law.
(b) A person who, at the time of committing the act, was unable to appreciate the nature or consequences of their actions due to insanity, mental disability, or involuntary intoxication, provided they did not voluntarily place themselves in such a state.
(c) A person who was coerced into committing the act by force or threat of imminent serious harm to themselves or their family.

Article 8:
A person shall not be held criminally responsible for an act committed in the following circumstances:
(a) Self-defense: The use of force necessary to repel an imminent unlawful aggression against the person, their property, or another person's life or property, provided the force used is proportionate to the threat.
(b) Performance of duty: An act committed in good faith in the execution of a legal obligation or a lawful order from a competent authority.
(c) Exercise of right: An act committed in the legitimate exercise of a right recognized by law.

Article 9:
Consent of the victim shall not constitute a justification for committing a crime unless the law provides otherwise. The commission of an offence against a person who has consented to the act shall not relieve the perpetrator of criminal responsibility except in cases explicitly provided for by law.

CHAPTER 3: Attempted Crimes and Accomplices

Article 10:
An attempt to commit a felony is punishable even if the intended result does not occur, provided the perpetrator has commenced the execution of the crime by acts that lead directly to its commission and was prevented from completing it by reasons beyond their control. Mere preparation for a crime shall not be considered an attempt unless the law provides otherwise.

Article 11:
The penalty for an attempted felony shall be as follows:
(a) If the penalty for the completed crime is the death penalty, the attempted crime shall be punished by life imprisonment.
(b) If the penalty for the completed crime is life imprisonment, the attempted crime shall be punished by temporary imprisonment for a period not exceeding ten years.
(c) In all other cases, the penalty shall not exceed half the maximum penalty prescribed for the completed crime.

Article 12:
Any person who participates in a crime as an accomplice shall be punished with the same penalty as the principal offender, unless the law provides otherwise. An accomplice is any person who:
(a) Incites or instigates another to commit a crime, and the crime is committed as a result of such incitement.
(b) Agrees with another to commit a crime, and the crime is committed pursuant to such agreement.
(c) Knowingly assists the principal offender in the acts preparatory to or facilitating the commission of the crime.
(d) Provides the principal offender with weapons, tools, or any other means used in the commission of the crime, knowing that they will be used for that purpose.

CHAPTER 4: Types of Penalties

Article 13:
Penalties shall be classified as follows:
(a) Original penalties: death penalty, life imprisonment, temporary imprisonment, confinement, detention, and fines.
(b) Accessory penalties: deprivation of certain rights and privileges, confiscation, closure of establishments, dissolution of legal entities, and publication of the judgment.
(c) Substitute penalties: community service, electronic monitoring, and any other measure prescribed by law.

Article 14:
Temporary imprisonment shall be for a term not less than three years and not exceeding fifteen years, unless the law provides otherwise. Imprisonment shall be served in the places designated by law. The convicted person shall be required to perform work assigned to them in accordance with the applicable regulations.

Article 15:
A fine is the obligation to pay the amount specified in the judgment to the State Treasury. A fine for a felony shall not be less than AED 100000. A fine for a misdemeanor shall not be less than AED 1000 and not more than AED 500000 unless the law provides otherwise. A fine for a violation shall not exceed AED 10000.

CHAPTER 5: Aggravating and Mitigating Circumstances

Article 16:
The following are aggravating circumstances that may increase the penalty:
(a) Recidivism: If the offender has been previously convicted of a felony or misdemeanor and commits a similar offence within five years of serving or being pardoned from the previous sentence.
(b) Commission of the crime by a public official in abuse of their position.
(c) Commission of the crime against a vulnerable person, including children, the elderly, or persons with disabilities.
(d) Commission of the crime with premeditation and deliberation.
(e) Commission of the crime using poisonous or explosive substances.

Article 17:
The court may, in its discretion, reduce the penalty below the minimum prescribed by law if it finds mitigating circumstances, provided that:
(a) If the penalty prescribed is the death penalty, it may be reduced to life imprisonment or temporary imprisonment for a term not less than seven years.
(b) If the penalty prescribed is life imprisonment, it may be reduced to temporary imprisonment for a term not less than three years.
(c) If the penalty prescribed is temporary imprisonment, it may be reduced to confinement for a term not less than six months.
(d) If the penalty prescribed is confinement, it may be reduced to a fine.

Article 18:
The court may suspend the execution of the penalty for a period not exceeding three years if the convicted person has not been previously sentenced to imprisonment for a felony or misdemeanor, and the court believes that the circumstances of the crime and the character of the offender justify such suspension. If the convicted person commits a new crime during the suspension period, the suspended penalty shall be enforced.

PART TWO: CRIMES AND PENALTIES

CHAPTER 1: Crimes Against Life and Bodily Integrity

Article 19:
Whoever intentionally and with premeditation kills another person shall be punished with the death penalty. Premeditation means a calm and deliberate intent formed before the commission of the act, with the purpose of taking the victim's life. The element of premeditation distinguishes murder from other forms of unlawful killing.

Article 20:
Whoever intentionally kills another person without premeditation shall be punished by life imprisonment or temporary imprisonment for a term not less than seven years. The absence of premeditation does not diminish the gravity of the offence but may be considered a mitigating factor in sentencing.

Article 21:
Whoever unintentionally causes the death of another person through negligence, recklessness, failure to observe laws and regulations, or lack of skill in their profession shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years and a fine not exceeding AED 200000, or by either of these penalties. If the act results from a serious violation of traffic regulations, the penalty shall be imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years.

Article 22:
Whoever intentionally assaults another person and causes them permanent disability, such as loss of sight, hearing, speech, or a limb, or permanent disfigurement, shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not less than five years and not exceeding ten years. If the assault results in permanent disability constituting a loss of a major bodily function, the penalty shall be imprisonment for a term not less than seven years.

Article 23:
Whoever intentionally assaults another person and causes them injury or illness that renders them unable to perform their personal activities for a period exceeding twenty days shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years and a fine not exceeding AED 100000, or by either penalty.

Article 24:
Whoever intentionally assaults another person causing injury or illness that does not exceed twenty days of incapacity shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year and a fine not exceeding AED 10000, or by either penalty. If the assault involves the use of a weapon or dangerous instrument, the penalty shall be doubled.

Article 25:
Whoever by negligence, recklessness, or failure to observe regulations causes bodily harm to another person that results in incapacity exceeding twenty days shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year and a fine not exceeding AED 50000, or by either penalty.

Article 26:
Whoever administers poison or a harmful substance to another person with intent to cause harm but not death, resulting in illness or incapacity, shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not less than three years and not exceeding ten years. If the administration of poison results in the death of the victim, the penalty shall be life imprisonment or the death penalty.

CHAPTER 2: Crimes Against Honor and Reputation

Article 27:
Whoever attributes to another person, by any means of publicity, an act that would subject them to punishment or contempt by others shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years and a fine not exceeding AED 50000, or by either penalty. If the defamation is directed at a public official in connection with their duties, the penalty shall be imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years and a fine not exceeding AED 100000.

Article 28:
Whoever insults another person by attributing to them a defect or condition that would diminish their standing, without specifying a particular act, shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months and a fine not exceeding AED 10000, or by either penalty.

Article 29:
Whoever publicly insults another person, whether by word, gesture, writing, or electronic means, in a manner that affects their dignity or honor shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year and a fine not exceeding AED 20000. The penalty shall be doubled if the insult is directed at a public official during or because of the performance of their duties.

Article 30:
Defamation and insult committed through electronic means, social media platforms, or information technology systems shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years and a fine not less than AED 250000 and not exceeding AED 500000, or by either penalty.

Article 31:
The truth of the imputed act shall be a defense in cases of defamation against a public official if the act relates to the performance of their public duties and the accused proves the truth of the imputation. In all other cases, proof of truth shall not constitute a defense.

CHAPTER 3: Crimes Against Personal Liberty

Article 32:
Whoever kidnaps, detains, or confines a person without legal authority shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not less than five years and not exceeding ten years and a fine not less than AED 200000. The penalty shall be life imprisonment if the kidnapping is accompanied by:
(a) The use of force, threat, or deception.
(b) The victim being a minor or a person with a disability.
(c) The purpose of extortion, ransom, or sexual exploitation.

Article 33:
Whoever threatens another person with harm to their life, property, or reputation, whether verbally, in writing, or through electronic means, in order to compel them to do or refrain from doing something shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years and a fine not exceeding AED 100000. If the threat is accompanied by a demand for money or a benefit, the penalty shall be imprisonment for a term not less than two years and not exceeding five years.

Article 34:
Whoever unlawfully enters or remains in the residence of another person against the will of the occupant shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year and a fine not exceeding AED 20000, or by either penalty. If the entry is committed at night or by the use of force, the penalty shall be imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years.

Article 35:
Whoever compels another person by force, threat, or exploitation of a position of authority to engage in forced labor or servitude shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not less than five years and not exceeding fifteen years and a fine not less than AED 500000. This article reflects the State's commitment to combating human trafficking and forced labor.

CHAPTER 4: Crimes Against Property

Article 36:
Whoever steals movable property belonging to another person shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not less than one year and not exceeding three years and a fine not exceeding AED 50000. Theft requires the taking of the property of another without consent, with the intent to permanently deprive the owner of it.

Article 37:
The penalty for theft shall be imprisonment for a term not less than three years and not exceeding seven years if the theft is committed in any of the following circumstances:
(a) At night or in an uninhabited place.
(b) By two or more persons.
(c) By a person carrying a weapon, whether visible or concealed.
(d) By breaking into, climbing over, or using forged keys to enter a place.
(e) By a servant or employee against their employer or in their employer's premises.
(f) By the use of violence or threat against the victim.

Article 38:
Whoever commits robbery, which is the theft of property by use of force or threat of force against the person of the victim, shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not less than five years and not exceeding ten years. If the robbery results in injury to the victim, the penalty shall be imprisonment for a term not less than seven years and not exceeding fifteen years. If the robbery results in the death of the victim, the penalty shall be life imprisonment or the death penalty.

Article 39:
Whoever obtains for themselves or for another person money, a movable property, a document, or a signature on a document by means of fraud, deception, or the assumption of a false identity shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years and a fine not exceeding AED 100000. If the fraud is committed against a public entity or involves public funds, the penalty shall be imprisonment for a term not less than two years and not exceeding five years and a fine not less than AED 200000.

Article 40:
Whoever embezzles money, documents, or property entrusted to them by virtue of their employment, profession, or official capacity shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years and a fine not exceeding AED 200000. If the embezzlement is committed by a public official, the penalty shall be imprisonment for a term not less than three years and not exceeding ten years and a fine not less than AED 500000.

Article 41:
Whoever breaches trust by misappropriating, wasting, or damaging money or property delivered to them under a contract of lease, pledge, loan for use, deposit, or agency shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years and a fine not exceeding AED 100000.

Article 42:
Whoever forges an official document, whether by alteration, fabrication, or counterfeiting, shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not less than three years and not exceeding ten years and a fine not less than AED 100000. Whoever forges a private document shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years and a fine not exceeding AED 50000. Whoever knowingly uses a forged document shall be punished with the same penalty as the forger.

Article 43:
Whoever intentionally destroys, damages, or renders useless movable or immovable property belonging to another person shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years and a fine not exceeding AED 50000, or by either penalty. If the damage is committed by fire, explosives, or flooding, the penalty shall be imprisonment for a term not less than three years and not exceeding ten years.

CHAPTER 5: Crimes Against Public Order

Article 44:
Whoever is found in a state of intoxication in a public place or causes a disturbance due to intoxication shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months and a fine not exceeding AED 5000, or by either penalty. The consumption of alcoholic beverages is prohibited unless in licensed premises in accordance with the applicable regulations.

Article 45:
Whoever is found gambling or operating a gambling establishment or online gambling platform shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years and a fine not exceeding AED 100000. The equipment and funds used in or derived from gambling shall be confiscated.

Article 46:
Whoever begs in public places shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months and a fine not exceeding AED 5000, or by either penalty. If the begging involves the exploitation of a minor, an elderly person, or a person with a disability, the penalty shall be imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year and a fine not exceeding AED 50000.

Article 47:
Whoever publicly incites others to commit crimes or advocates for ideas that undermine public order or national unity shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years and a fine not exceeding AED 500000. The penalty shall be increased if the incitement occurs through electronic means or social media platforms.

Article 48:
Whoever establishes, organizes, or manages an unlicensed association, organization, or gathering that aims to undermine public order or security shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not less than one year and not exceeding five years. Members who join such organizations with knowledge of their purposes shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years.

CHAPTER 6: Drug Offenses

Article 49:
Whoever imports, exports, manufactures, or produces narcotic or psychotropic substances with the intent to traffic shall be punished by life imprisonment and a fine not less than AED 500000 and not exceeding AED 5000000. The court may order the deportation of the convicted person if they are a non-national upon completion of the sentence.

Article 50:
Whoever possesses narcotic or psychotropic substances with the intent to sell, distribute, or traffic shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not less than ten years and not exceeding fifteen years and a fine not less than AED 200000 and not exceeding AED 1000000.

Article 51:
Whoever possesses narcotic or psychotropic substances for personal use shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not less than one year and not exceeding three years and a fine not less than AED 20000 and not exceeding AED 100000. The court may, instead of imposing a penalty, order that the person be admitted to a treatment and rehabilitation center for a period not exceeding one year, if the person is a first-time offender and is addicted to the substance.

Article 52:
Whoever uses or consumes narcotic or psychotropic substances shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not less than six months and not exceeding two years and a fine not less than AED 10000 and not exceeding AED 50000. A person who voluntarily presents themselves for treatment before being apprehended shall not be subject to criminal prosecution for personal use.

Article 53:
Whoever facilitates or prepares a place for the use of narcotic or psychotropic substances by others shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not less than five years and not exceeding ten years and a fine not less than AED 100000. If the offender exploits a minor or a person with a mental disability, the penalty shall be doubled.

Article 54:
The following substances are classified as narcotic and psychotropic substances for the purposes of this Law: cannabis, hashish, cocaine, heroin, methamphetamine, amphetamine, MDMA, LSD, synthetic cannabinoids, and any substance listed in the schedules attached to Federal Law No. 14 of 1995 and its amendments.

CHAPTER 7: Crimes Against the Family

Article 55:
Whoever commits adultery shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year. Prosecution for adultery shall not be initiated except upon a complaint filed by the spouse. The complaint may be withdrawn at any time before the issuance of a final judgment, and such withdrawal shall result in the dismissal of the case. If the complainant resumes marital relations with the accused, the right to file a complaint shall be forfeited.

Article 56:
Whoever abandons or neglects to provide maintenance for a person they are legally obligated to maintain, including a spouse, minor children, or elderly parents, despite having the financial means to do so, shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year and a fine not exceeding AED 50000, or by either penalty.

Article 57:
Whoever unlawfully takes or conceals a minor from the custody of the person legally entitled to their custody shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years and a fine not exceeding AED 100000, or by either penalty. If the perpetrator is a parent of the minor, the penalty shall be imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year.

Article 58:
Whoever exposes a child under the age of seven to danger by abandoning them in a deserted place or by failing to provide them with necessary care, food, or shelter shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years and a fine not exceeding AED 50000. If the abandonment results in the death or permanent disability of the child, the penalty shall be imprisonment for a term not less than five years.

Article 59:
Whoever commits domestic violence, including physical, psychological, or economic abuse against a family member, shall be punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year and a fine not exceeding AED 50000, or by either penalty. The penalty shall be doubled if the violence is committed against a minor, an elderly person, or a person with a disability, or if the offender is in a position of authority over the victim.

PART THREE: SUPPLEMENTARY PROVISIONS

Article 60:
The penalties prescribed in this Law shall not prejudice any civil liability arising from the criminal act. The injured party shall retain the right to claim compensation for damages before the competent civil courts.

Article 61:
The provisions of this Law shall be interpreted in a manner consistent with the Constitution of the United Arab Emirates, Islamic Sharia principles, and the State's international obligations under ratified treaties and conventions.

Article 62:
Any provision of Federal Law No. 3 of 1987 Concerning the Penal Code, and any other provision that contradicts or conflicts with the provisions of this Law, shall be repealed. Regulations and decisions implementing repealed provisions shall remain in force to the extent they do not conflict with this Law, until new implementing regulations are issued.

Article 63:
The Minister of Justice shall issue the implementing regulations and decisions necessary for the enforcement of this Law within six months of its publication in the Official Gazette.

Article 64:
This Law shall be published in the Official Gazette and shall come into force on January 2, 2022, which is three months after the date of its issuance on October 2, 2021.
